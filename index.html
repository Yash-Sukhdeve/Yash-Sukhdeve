about.md

I'm a PhD student at CITER (Center for Identification Technology Research), Clarkson University. My research focuses on voice biometrics, speaker verification, and presentation attack detection.

I develop robust anti-spoofing systems to protect biometric authentication against deepfakes and synthetic speech. My work combines signal processing, deep learning, and audio forensics to advance the security of identity verification systems.
